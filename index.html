YOUR TURN

  1. Export a guide you like.
  2. Attach the motion guide and your original character still, in either order.
  3. Paste the prompt into your image generator.
  4. Slice the result and play it at the exported timing.

Keep your original character still as the appearance reference. A previous animation can carry its mistakes into the next one.
